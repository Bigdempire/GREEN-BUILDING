What is embodied carbon?

Embodied carbon measures total emissions over the lifecycle of a material, including extraction, manufacturing, transport, and installation.

Why choose low-carbon materials?

Low-carbon alternatives reduce operational emissions and improve material resiliency — helping projects qualify for green certifications.
